# Improved EV Database Plan: Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive, improved plan for creating an Electric Vehicle (EV) database with specific focus on the Portuguese and Spanish markets, supporting three languages: Portuguese, Spanish, and English. The plan addresses the gaps identified in the original proposal and provides a detailed roadmap for implementation.

## Key Enhancements to Original Plan

1. **Market-Specific Analysis**: Detailed research on both Portuguese and Spanish EV markets, including current trends, growth projections, key players, and government incentives.

2. **Multilingual Framework**: Comprehensive requirements for supporting Portuguese, Spanish, and English throughout the database, including technical considerations for character encoding, date/number formatting, and translation workflows.

3. **Enhanced Database Structure**: Robust database schema designed specifically for multilingual content and regional market data, with clear entity relationships and validation rules.

4. **Validated Data Sources**: Thoroughly vetted data sources for both markets, with detailed collection methods and validation processes.

5. **Detailed Implementation Roadmap**: 12-month phased implementation plan with clear milestones, resource allocation, and success metrics.

## Key Findings

### Portuguese Market
- BEVs secured 19.91% market share in 2024
- Total EV sales reached 70,087 units in 2024 (10.31% annual increase)
- Tesla leads the market with more than double its closest competitor
- Strong renewable energy integration (81%) supporting EV adoption

### Spanish Market
- 46,760 EV units sold in 2024 (33.2% increase from 2023)
- BEV market share reached 6.9% in Q1 2025
- MOVES III program provides substantial incentives (up to €7,000)
- Projected to reach USD 8.20 billion by 2029 (11.4% CAGR)

### Multilingual Considerations
- Specific character encoding requirements for Portuguese and Spanish
- Different date and number formatting conventions
- Translation management workflow for ongoing content updates
- Language-specific search and filtering capabilities

## Implementation Approach

The implementation is structured in four phases over 12 months:

1. **Foundation** (Months 1-3): Database setup, core data collection, multilingual framework
2. **Market-Specific Features** (Months 4-6): Portuguese and Spanish market modules, cross-market integration
3. **User Experience** (Months 7-9): Interface development, interactive features, user-generated content
4. **Optimization and Launch** (Months 10-12): Performance tuning, testing, and deployment
