# Comparative EV Market Analysis: Portugal and Spain

## Market Size and Growth

### Portugal
- **2024 Total EV Sales**: 70,087 units (10.31% annual increase)
  - BEVs: 41,741 units (+14.7% YoY)
  - PHEVs: 28,346 units (+4.5% YoY)
- **BEV Market Share**: 19.91% of light passenger vehicles
- **2025 Projection**: Revenue of US$4.9bn
- **Long-term Forecast**: 73,000 units by 2028 (from ~40,000 in 2023)
- **Growth Rate**: Projected CAGR of 16.47% (2025-2029)

### Spain
- **2024 Total EV Sales**: 46,760 units (33.2% increase from 2023)
- **Overall Auto Market**: 1.014 million total vehicle sales (+7.1% YoY)
- **Q1 2025 EV Performance**: 
  - 39,737 EVs registered (+46.7% YoY)
  - 14.2% market share (up from 11.1%)
- **2025 Projection**: USD 5.33 billion (14.6% annual increase)
- **Long-term Forecast**: ~222,000 units by 2028 (from ~110,000 in 2023)
- **Growth Rate**: Projected CAGR of 11.4% (2025-2029)

## Market Leaders

### Portugal
1. **Tesla**: Dominant leader with 9,760 BEVs (more than double its closest competitor)
2. **BMW**: 4,074 units
3. **Volvo**: 3,203 units
4. **Peugeot**: 3,017 units
5. **Mercedes-Benz**: 2,860 units

### Spain
1. **Tesla**: Market leader
2. **BYD**: Rapid growth (757.2% YoY), rising to second position
3. **Volvo**: Strong performance (5th position)
4. **Fiat**: Strong performance (9th position)

## Government Incentives

### Portugal
- Strong government commitment to EV transition
- Continued policy support driving adoption
- Specific incentive structures not fully detailed in research

### Spain
- **MOVES III Program**: Extended to end of 2025
  - €7,000 subsidy for BEVs/PHEVs with vehicle scrapping
  - €5,500 without scrapping
  - €5,000 for PHEVs with 30-90km electric range
- **Tax Incentives**: 15% personal income tax reduction (up to €20,000)
- **Total Funding**: Over €1.7 million (€400 million recently added)

## Infrastructure Development

### Portugal
- Rapid installation of new charging stations nationwide
- 81% renewable energy integration into the grid
- 34% growth in public charging infrastructure in 2023

### Spain
- Significant focus on charging infrastructure development
- Multiple charging types being deployed (AC/DC, various speeds)
- Growing emphasis on smart charging stations

## Market Challenges

### Portugal
- Need for further charging infrastructure expansion
- Maintaining EV affordability
- Decline in commercial electric vehicle adoption:
  - LCVs: 2,340 units (-4.8%)
  - Heavy vehicles: 123 units (-69.2%)

### Spain
- Despite growth, remains behind other major European markets in volume
- Continued need for charging infrastructure expansion
- Declining diesel segment (-28.4% in March 2025)

## Comparative Insights

### Market Maturity
- **Portugal**: Higher BEV market penetration (19.91% vs. Spain's ~7%)
- **Spain**: Larger overall market volume but lower EV penetration

### Growth Patterns
- **Portugal**: Steady growth with strong BEV dominance
- **Spain**: More rapid recent growth but from a lower base

### Policy Approach
- **Spain**: More detailed and structured incentive programs
- **Portugal**: Strong commitment but less detailed incentive information available

### Consumer Preferences
- **Both Markets**: Tesla leadership indicates similar premium brand preferences
- **Spain**: Stronger hybrid adoption (42.1% market share in March 2025)

## Multilingual Market Considerations

### Spanish Market
- Requires Spanish language support for consumer-facing information

- Localized pricing reflecting Spanish tax incentives and regional variations
