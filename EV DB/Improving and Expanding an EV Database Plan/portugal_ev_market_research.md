# Portugal EV Market Research

## Market Overview and Key Statistics

### 2024 Performance
- **Total EV Sales**: 70,087 units in 2024 (10.31% annual increase)
  - **Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs)**: 41,741 units (+14.7% YoY)
  - **Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs)**: 28,346 units (+4.5% YoY)
- **Market Share**: BEVs secured 19.91% market share of light passenger vehicles
- **Monthly Record**: December 2024 saw over 10,000 EVs registered in a single month
  - 7,059 BEVs
  - 3,383 PHEVs
- **Growth Trajectory**: BEVs became the top-selling category in Portugal's light passenger vehicle market in September 2024

### 2025 Trends
- **January 2025**: BEVs reached record 22.5% market share
- **Projected Growth**: According to Statista, the Electric Vehicles market in Portugal is projected to reach revenue of US$4.9bn in 2025
- **Future Projection**: EV registrations expected to reach approximately 73,000 units by 2028 (from around 40,000 in 2023)

### Commercial Vehicle Segment
- **Light Commercial Vehicles (LCVs)**: 2,340 units registered in 2024 (4.8% decline from 2023)
  - Fully electric LCVs: 2,323 units (5.1% drop)
- **Heavy Vehicles**: Only 123 electrified units registered in 2024 (69.2% decline from 2023)

## Infrastructure and Renewable Energy

- **Charging Network**: Rapid installation of new charging stations nationwide
- **Renewable Energy Integration**: Reached milestone of 81% integration into the grid
- **Charging Infrastructure Growth**: According to Roland Berger, Portugal's public charging infrastructure grew by 34% in 2023

## Government Policies and Incentives

- Portugal has shown strong commitment to transitioning to electric mobility
- Policy support continues to drive EV adoption
- Specific incentive structures need further research

## Market Challenges

- Need for further expansion of charging infrastructure capacity
- Maintaining affordability of EVs
- Decline in commercial electric vehicle adoption (both LCVs and heavy vehicles)

## Future Outlook

- Momentum expected to continue into 2025 and beyond
- New EV models entering the market
- Further policy support anticipated
- Continued public and private sector collaboration
- Projected CAGR (2025-2029): 16.47% according to Statista, resulting in a market volume of €8.8bn by 2029
