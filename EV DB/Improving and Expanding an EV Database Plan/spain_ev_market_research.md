# Spain EV Market Research

## Market Overview and Key Statistics

### 2024 Performance
- **Total EV Sales**: 46,760 units in 2024 (33.2% increase compared to 2023)
- **Market Growth**: Spanish auto market grew 7.1% in 2024, reaching 1.014 million total vehicle sales
- **Market Recovery**: First time the market has surpassed 1 million sales since the pandemic
- **EV Market Leaders**: 
  - Tesla continued to lead the market
  - BYD experienced impressive 757.2% growth, rising to second position
  - Volvo and Fiat also delivered strong performances (5th and 9th respectively)

### 2025 Trends (Q1)
- **March 2025**: 116,725 new cars registered (23.1% increase year-on-year)
- **Q1 2025**: 279,368 total registrations (14.1% improvement)
- **BEV Performance**: 
  - March 2025: 8,101 BEVs registered (92.7% increase year-on-year)
  - Q1 2025: 19,225 BEVs registered (68.8% increase, 7,839 more units than Q1 2024)
  - Market share: 6.9% in Q1 2025 (up from 4.6% in Q1 2024)
- **PHEV Performance**:
  - March 2025: 8,369 PHEVs registered (50.6% increase year-on-year)
  - Q1 2025: 20,512 PHEVs registered (30.7% increase)
  - Market share: 7.3% in Q1 2025 (up from 6.4% in Q1 2024)
- **Combined EV Market**:
  - March 2025: 16,470 EVs registered (68.7% increase)
  - Q1 2025: 39,737 EVs registered (46.7% increase, 12,651 more units than Q1 2024)
  - Market share: 14.2% in Q1 2025 (up from 11.1% in Q1 2024)

### Future Projections
- **Market Growth**: Projected to reach USD 5.33 billion by 2025 (14.6% annual increase)
- **Historical Growth**: 18.4% CAGR from 2020 to 2024
- **Future Forecast**: Expected to reach USD 8.20 billion by 2029 (11.4% CAGR from 2025-2029)
- **EV Registrations**: Projected to more than double by 2028, from approximately 110,000 units in 2023 to around 222,000 units

## Government Policies and Incentives

### MOVES III Program
- **Extension**: Retroactively extended to the end of 2025 (effective from January 1, 2025)
- **Subsidy Amount**: 
  - €7,000 when purchasing a BEV, PHEV, or hydrogen fuel-cell vehicle (with vehicle scrapping)
  - €5,500 without scrapping a vehicle over 10 years old
  - Up to €5,000 for PHEVs with electric range of 30km to 90km
- **Funding**: Additional €400 million contributed, raising total funds to over €1.7 million

### Tax Incentives
- **Personal Income Tax Reduction**: 15% reduction of up to €20,000 when buying a plug-in model
- **Impact**: These incentives are expected to boost EV adoption after a reported 50% drop in orders for electrified vehicles due to previous uncertainty about subsidies

## Market Challenges and Opportunities

### Challenges
- **Diesel Decline**: Worst-performing major powertrain in March 2025 (-28.4%)
- **Market Position**: Despite growth, Spain remains behind France, Germany, and Italy in total volume
- **Infrastructure Development**: Continued need for charging infrastructure expansion

### Opportunities
- **Hybrid Growth**: Hybrids led Spain's new-car market in March 2025 with 49,095 units (+44.8%)
- **Electrified Dominance**: Combined electrified market (hybrids + EVs) captured 56.2% of the market in March 2025
- **Declining ICE**: Petrol vehicles saw a 9.5% decline in March 2025, creating space for EV growth

## Infrastructure Development

- **Charging Infrastructure Market**: Expected to grow significantly as part of the overall EV ecosystem
- **Infrastructure Types**: 
  - Alternating Current (AC) and Direct Current (DC)
  - Fixed and Portable Installation Types
  - Various charging speeds (Slow, Fast, Rapid, Ultrarapid)
  - Multiple connector types (CSS, CHAdeMO, GB/T, etc.)
- **Smart Charging**: Growing focus on connected charging stations vs. non-connected stations
